## Project Showcase
### Education, Salary and Working Hours Data Analysis
**Research Question**: How does education level influence people's annual salary and weekly working hours?

This project uses public cross-sectional data to explore the relationship between education attainment, annual salary and weekly working hours. I created treemap for education group proportions and violin plots to show salary distribution across education levels. The analysis shows higher education generally links to higher salary, but no strong correlation between education and working hours. One limitation is cross-sectional data cannot prove causation.
